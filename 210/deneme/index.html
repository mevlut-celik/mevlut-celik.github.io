    Sınavda verilecek türden formül sayfası

    Nasıl kullanılır? Denemeyi çözerken bu kartlara bakmakta serbestsin; gerçek sınavda da önünde olacaklar. Asıl iş, hangi \(h_i\)'nin nereye gireceğini ve normalin yönünü doğru seçmek.

    İndis

    \[ \epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{imn}=\delta_{jm}\delta_{kn}-\delta_{jn}\delta_{km} \] \[ (\mathbf A\times\mathbf B)_i=\epsilon_{ijk}A_jB_k,\quad (\nabla\times\mathbf A)_i=\epsilon_{ijk}\partial_jA_k \]

    Uzay eğrisi

    \[ \frac{d\mathbf T}{ds}=\kappa\mathbf N,\ \ \frac{d\mathbf N}{ds}=-\kappa\mathbf T+\tau\mathbf B,\ \ \frac{d\mathbf B}{ds}=-\tau\mathbf N \] \[ \kappa=\frac{|\dot{\mathbf r}\times\ddot{\mathbf r}|}{|\dot{\mathbf r}|^3},\qquad \tau=\frac{(\dot{\mathbf r}\times\ddot{\mathbf r})\cdot\dddot{\mathbf r}}{|\dot{\mathbf r}\times\ddot{\mathbf r}|^2} \]

    Dik eğrisel: elemanlar

    \[ h_i=\Big|\frac{\partial\mathbf r}{\partial u_i}\Big|,\quad ds^2=\sum h_i^2\,du_i^2,\quad d\tau=h_1h_2h_3\,du_1du_2du_3 \]

    Silindirik \((1,\ r,\ 1)\) · küresel \((1,\ r,\ r\sin\theta)\).

    Dik eğrisel: gradyan, diverjans

    \[ \nabla f=\sum_i\frac{1}{h_i}\frac{\partial f}{\partial u_i}\hat{\mathbf e}_i \] \[ \nabla\cdot\mathbf V=\frac{1}{h_1h_2h_3}\Big[\partial_1(h_2h_3V_1)+\partial_2(h_3h_1V_2)+\partial_3(h_1h_2V_3)\Big] \]

    Dik eğrisel: rotasyonel, Laplace

    \[ \nabla\times\mathbf V=\frac{1}{h_1h_2h_3}\begin{vmatrix}h_1\hat{\mathbf e}_1&h_2\hat{\mathbf e}_2&h_3\hat{\mathbf e}_3\\ \partial_1&\partial_2&\partial_3\\ h_1V_1&h_2V_2&h_3V_3\end{vmatrix} \] \[ \nabla^2f=\frac{1}{h_1h_2h_3}\sum_i\partial_i\Big(\frac{h_1h_2h_3}{h_i^2}\,\partial_if\Big) \]

    Yüzey ve teoremler

    \[ g(x,y,z)=0:\ \ \hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=\frac{\nabla g}{|\partial_zg|}\,dx\,dy \]

    Diverjans (kapalı yüzey, dış normal):

    \[ \oint_S\mathbf V\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=\int_V\nabla\cdot\mathbf V\,d\tau \]

    Stokes (yol yönü ile normal sağ el kuralıyla):

    \[ \oint_C\mathbf V\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_S(\nabla\times\mathbf V)\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma \]

    Vectors

    Q1

    (a) Using index notation, prove \(\nabla\cdot(\mathbf A\times\mathbf B)=\mathbf B\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf A)-\mathbf A\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\).

    (b) Find the volume of the parallelepiped with edges \(\mathbf a=(1,2,-1)\), \(\mathbf b=(2,0,1)\), \(\mathbf c=(0,1,3)\).

    (c) Find the rate of change of \(\varphi=x^2y-z\) at \((1,1,2)\) in the direction of \(\mathbf a\).

    Neden bu soru? “Vektörler” başlığı geniş; indis kanıtı, üçlü çarpım ve gradyanın üçü de bu başlıkta. Kısa parçalarla hepsini yokluyor.

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    Çarpım kuralı ve \(\epsilon\)'nin döngüsel simetrisi

    1. (a) \(\partial_i(\epsilon_{ijk}A_jB_k)=\epsilon_{ijk}(\partial_iA_j)B_k+\epsilon_{ijk}A_j(\partial_iB_k)\).

    2. Birinci terim: \(\epsilon_{ijk}=\epsilon_{kij}\) ⇒ \(B_k\,\epsilon_{kij}\partial_iA_j=B_k(\nabla\times\mathbf A)_k\). İkinci terim: \(\epsilon_{ijk}=-\epsilon_{jik}\) ⇒ \(-A_j\,\epsilon_{jik}\partial_iB_k=-A_j(\nabla\times\mathbf B)_j\). Toplam \(\boxed{\mathbf B\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf A)-\mathbf A\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf B)}\).

    3. (b) \(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c=(0\cdot3-1\cdot1,\ 1\cdot0-2\cdot3,\ 2\cdot1-0)=(-1,-6,2)\); \(\mathbf a\cdot(\mathbf b\times\mathbf c)=-1-12-2=-15\). Hacim \(\boxed{15}\) (mutlak değer).

    4. (c) \(\nabla\varphi=(2xy,\ x^2,\ -1)=(2,1,-1)\), \(\hat{\mathbf a}=(1,2,-1)/\sqrt6\): \(\nabla\varphi\cdot\hat{\mathbf a}=(2+2+1)/\sqrt6=\boxed{5/\sqrt6}\approx2{,}04\).

    • (a) bileşen yazımı 3, çarpım kuralı 2, iki indis kaydırması 3 · (b) çapraz çarpım 3, sonuç ve mutlak değer 2 · (c) gradyan 3, birim vektör 2, sonuç 2

    Parabolic cylindrical coordinates

    Q2

    Parabolic cylindrical coordinates \((u,v,z)\) are defined by \(x=\tfrac12(u^2-v^2)\), \(y=uv\), \(z=z\).

    (a) Show that the system is orthogonal. (b) Find the scale factors, the unit vectors \(\hat{\mathbf e}_u,\hat{\mathbf e}_v\) in terms of \(\hat{\mathbf i},\hat{\mathbf j}\), and the volume element. (c) For \(\Phi=u^2+v^2\) find \(\nabla\Phi\) and \(\nabla^2\Phi\), and check \(\nabla^2\Phi\) in Cartesian coordinates.

    Neden bu soru? Standart dışı bir sistem verip formül sayfasındaki genel ifadeleri kullandırmak, “formül veriyorum” diyen hocanın en olası sorusu.

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    \(h_u=h_v=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\), \(h_z=1\)

    1. (a) \(\partial_u\mathbf r=(u,\ v,\ 0)\), \(\partial_v\mathbf r=(-v,\ u,\ 0)\), \(\partial_z\mathbf r=(0,0,1)\). Nokta çarpımlar: \(-uv+uv=0\), diğer ikisi de 0 ⇒ dik.

    2. (b) \(h_u=h_v=\sqrt{u^2+v^2}\), \(h_z=1\). \(\hat{\mathbf e}_u=\dfrac{u\,\hat{\mathbf i}+v\,\hat{\mathbf j}}{\sqrt{u^2+v^2}}\), \(\hat{\mathbf e}_v=\dfrac{-v\,\hat{\mathbf i}+u\,\hat{\mathbf j}}{\sqrt{u^2+v^2}}\). \(\boxed{d\tau=(u^2+v^2)\,du\,dv\,dz}\).

    3. (c) \(\nabla\Phi=\dfrac{1}{h_u}2u\,\hat{\mathbf e}_u+\dfrac{1}{h_v}2v\,\hat{\mathbf e}_v=\dfrac{2(u\,\hat{\mathbf e}_u+v\,\hat{\mathbf e}_v)}{\sqrt{u^2+v^2}}\).

    4. \(h_uh_vh_z/h_u^2=1\) olduğundan \(\nabla^2\Phi=\dfrac{1}{u^2+v^2}(\Phi_{uu}+\Phi_{vv})=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{u^2+v^2}}\).

    5. Kontrol: \(x^2+y^2=\tfrac14(u^2+v^2)^2\) ⇒ \(\Phi=2\rho\) (\(\rho=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\)). Silindirikte \(\nabla^2(2\rho)=\tfrac{2}{\rho}=\tfrac{4}{u^2+v^2}\) ✓.

    • (a) teğet vektörler 3, diklik 2 · (b) ölçek çarpanları 4, birim vektörler 2, \(d\tau\) 2 · (c) gradyan 2, Laplace formülünü doğru kurmak 3, kontrol 2
    A · Q3yüzey20 puan

    A paraboloidal bowl

    Q3

    \(S\) is the part of the paraboloid \(z=x^2+y^2\) with \(0\le z\le1\).

    (a) Find the area of \(S\). (b) Find the flux of \(\mathbf F=x\,\hat{\mathbf i}+y\,\hat{\mathbf j}+z\,\hat{\mathbf k}\) through \(S\), with the normal having a positive \(z\)-component.

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    \(g=z-x^2-y^2\), \(xy\) düzlemine izdüşüm, kutupsal

    1. \(\nabla g=(-2x,\ -2y,\ 1)\), \(|\partial_zg|=1\) ⇒ \(\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=(-2x,-2y,1)\,dx\,dy\) (bu normalin \(z\) bileşeni pozitif), \(d\sigma=\sqrt{1+4\rho^2}\,dx\,dy\). İzdüşüm bölgesi \(\rho\le1\).

    2. (a) \(A=\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^1\sqrt{1+4\rho^2}\,\rho\,d\rho\,d\theta=2\pi\Big[\tfrac{(1+4\rho^2)^{3/2}}{12}\Big]_0^1=\boxed{\tfrac{\pi}{6}\big(5\sqrt5-1\big)}\approx5{,}33\).

    3. (b) \(\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma=(-2x^2-2y^2+z)\,dx\,dy=(-2\rho^2+\rho^2)\,dx\,dy=-\rho^2\,dx\,dy\).

    4. \(\Phi=-\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^1\rho^3\,d\rho\,d\theta=\boxed{-\tfrac{\pi}{2}}\).

    5. Kontrol (diverjansla): kapağı \(z=1\) diski olan katıda \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=3\), hacim \(\int_0^1\pi z\,dz=\pi/2\) ⇒ toplam dışa akı \(3\pi/2\). Disk \(\pi\) veriyor; paraboloidden dışa (aşağı) akı \(\pi/2\), yukarı yönle \(-\pi/2\) ✓.

    • \(\hat{\mathbf n}\,d\sigma\) ve yön 5 · (a) integral kurulumu 4, sonuç 3 · (b) integrand 4, sonuç 4
    A · Q4çizgi20 puan

    Work along a helix

    Q4

    \(C\) is the helix \(\mathbf r(t)=(\cos t,\ \sin t,\ t)\), \(0\le t\le\pi\).

    (a) Show that \(\mathbf F=yz\,\hat{\mathbf i}+xz\,\hat{\mathbf j}+(xy+2z)\,\hat{\mathbf k}\) is conservative, find its potential and evaluate \(\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r\).

    (b) For \(\mathbf G=-y\,\hat{\mathbf i}+x\,\hat{\mathbf j}\), evaluate \(\int\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r\) along \(C\) and along the straight segment with the same end points. Explain the difference.

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    Uçlar \((1,0,0)\to(-1,0,\pi)\)

    1. (a) \(\nabla\times\mathbf F=(x-x,\ y-y,\ z-z)=\mathbf 0\) ⇒ korunumlu. \(\varphi=xyz+\psi(z)\), \(\partial_z\varphi=xy+\psi'=xy+2z\) ⇒ \(\boxed{\varphi=xyz+z^2}\).

    2. \(\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\varphi(-1,0,\pi)-\varphi(1,0,0)=\boxed{\pi^2}\). (Doğrudan: integrand \(t\cos2t+\tfrac12\sin2t+2t\); ilk iki terim \([0,\pi]\)'de 0 verir.)

    3. (b) Sarmalda \(\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r=(-\sin t)(-\sin t)\,dt+\cos t\cos t\,dt=dt\) ⇒ \(\boxed{\pi}\).

    4. Doğru parça \((1-2s,\ 0,\ \pi s)\): \(y=0\), \(dy=0\) ⇒ \(\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r=0\) ⇒ \(\boxed0\).

    5. Fark, \(\nabla\times\mathbf G=2\hat{\mathbf k}\neq\mathbf 0\) olmasından: \(\mathbf G\) korunumlu değil, integral yola bağlı. \(\mathbf F\) için iki yol aynı sonucu verir.

    • (a) rotasyonel 3, potansiyel 4, sonuç 3 · (b) sarmal 4, doğru parça 3, açıklama 3
    A · Q5diverjans20 puan

    Divergence theorem

    Q5

    \(\mathbf F=x\,\hat{\mathbf i}+y\,\hat{\mathbf j}+z^2\,\hat{\mathbf k}\).

    (a) Verify the divergence theorem for the closed cylinder \(x^2+y^2\le a^2\), \(0\le z\le h\). (b) Use the divergence theorem to find the upward flux of \(\mathbf F\) through the hemisphere \(z=\sqrt{a^2-x^2-y^2}\).

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=2+2z\)

    1. (a) Hacim: \(\int(2+2z)\,d\tau=\pi a^2\int_0^h(2+2z)\,dz=\boxed{\pi a^2(2h+h^2)}\).

    2. Yan yüzey: \(\hat{\mathbf n}=(x,y,0)/a\), \(\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}=(x^2+y^2)/a=a\); alan \(2\pi ah\) ⇒ \(2\pi a^2h\).

    3. Üst kapak \(z=h\), \(\hat{\mathbf n}=\hat{\mathbf k}\): \(F_z=h^2\) ⇒ \(\pi a^2h^2\). Alt kapak \(z=0\), \(\hat{\mathbf n}=-\hat{\mathbf k}\): \(-z^2=0\) ⇒ 0. Toplam \(\pi a^2(2h+h^2)\) ✓.

    4. (b) Yarım küreyi \(z=0\) diskiyle kapat. Diskte \(\mathbf F\cdot(-\hat{\mathbf k})=-z^2=0\), yani tüm akı yarım küreden geçer.

    5. \(\int(2+2z)\,d\tau=2\cdot\tfrac23\pi a^3+2\int_0^a z\,\pi(a^2-z^2)\,dz=\tfrac43\pi a^3+2\cdot\tfrac{\pi a^4}{4}=\boxed{\tfrac43\pi a^3+\tfrac{\pi a^4}{2}}\).

    • (a) diverjans 2, hacim integrali 4, üç yüzey (yan 4, üst 2, alt 1) · (b) kapatma fikri 3, hacim integrali 4

    Vectors and a space curve

    Q1

    (a) Using index notation, prove \(\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf A)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf A)-\nabla^2\mathbf A\).

    (b) For the curve \(\mathbf r(t)=(2t,\ t^2,\ \ln t)\), \(t>0\): find the arc length from \(t=1\) to \(t=e\), and the unit tangent, the curvature and the torsion at \(t=1\).

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    \(\epsilon\epsilon\) özdeşliği; \(|\dot{\mathbf r}|=2t+1/t\)

    1. (a) \([\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf A)]_i=\epsilon_{ijk}\partial_j\epsilon_{kmn}\partial_mA_n=\epsilon_{kij}\epsilon_{kmn}\partial_j\partial_mA_n=(\delta_{im}\delta_{jn}-\delta_{in}\delta_{jm})\partial_j\partial_mA_n\).

    2. \(=\partial_i(\partial_jA_j)-\partial_j\partial_jA_i=\boxed{[\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf A)-\nabla^2\mathbf A]_i}\).

    3. (b) \(\dot{\mathbf r}=(2,\ 2t,\ 1/t)\), \(|\dot{\mathbf r}|^2=4+4t^2+1/t^2=(2t+1/t)^2\). Yay uzunluğu \(\int_1^e(2t+1/t)\,dt=(e^2-1)+1=\boxed{e^2}\).

    4. \(t=1\): \(\dot{\mathbf r}=(2,2,1)\), \(|\dot{\mathbf r}|=3\) ⇒ \(\mathbf T=\tfrac13(2,2,1)\). \(\ddot{\mathbf r}=(0,2,-1)\), \(\dddot{\mathbf r}=(0,0,2)\).

    5. \(\dot{\mathbf r}\times\ddot{\mathbf r}=(-4,\ 2,\ 4)\), boyu 6. \(\kappa=6/27=\boxed{2/9}\); \(\tau=(-4,2,4)\cdot(0,0,2)/36=\boxed{2/9}\).

    • (a) iki \(\epsilon\) yazımı 3, özdeşlik 3, sonuç 2 · (b) yay uzunluğu 4, \(\mathbf T\) 2, \(\kappa\) 3, \(\tau\) 3
    B · Q2eğrisel20 puan

    Spherical coordinates

    Q2

    (a) Starting from \(x=r\sin\theta\cos\phi\), \(y=r\sin\theta\sin\phi\), \(z=r\cos\theta\), show that the spherical system is orthogonal and find \(h_r,h_\theta,h_\phi\) and \(d\tau\).

    (b) Find \(\nabla\cdot(r^n\hat{\mathbf e}_r)\). For which \(n\) is it zero (\(r\ne0\))?

    (c) Find \(\nabla\times(r\sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf e}_\phi)\) and show that it equals \(2\hat{\mathbf k}\).

    (d) Find the volume of the region \(r\le a\), \(0\le\theta\le\pi/3\).

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    \(h=(1,\ r,\ r\sin\theta)\)

    1. (a) \(\partial_r\mathbf r=(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\ \sin\theta\sin\phi,\ \cos\theta)\), \(\partial_\theta\mathbf r=r(\cos\theta\cos\phi,\ \cos\theta\sin\phi,\ -\sin\theta)\), \(\partial_\phi\mathbf r=r\sin\theta(-\sin\phi,\ \cos\phi,\ 0)\). Üç nokta çarpım da 0. Boylar \(1,\ r,\ r\sin\theta\) ⇒ \(d\tau=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi\).

    2. (b) \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf V=\dfrac{1}{r^2\sin\theta}\partial_r(r^2\sin\theta\cdot r^n)=\boxed{(n+2)\,r^{n-1}}\); \(n=-2\) için sıfır (Coulomb alanı).

    3. (c) \(V_\phi=r\sin\theta\), \(h_\phi V_\phi=r^2\sin^2\theta\). Determinanttan: \((\nabla\times\mathbf V)_r=\dfrac{1}{r^2\sin\theta}\partial_\theta(r^2\sin^2\theta)=2\cos\theta\), \((\nabla\times\mathbf V)_\theta=-\dfrac{1}{r\sin\theta}\partial_r(r^2\sin^2\theta)=-2\sin\theta\).

    4. \(\nabla\times\mathbf V=2(\cos\theta\,\hat{\mathbf e}_r-\sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf e}_\theta)=\boxed{2\hat{\mathbf k}}\). Kontrol: \(r\sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf e}_\phi=-y\,\hat{\mathbf i}+x\,\hat{\mathbf j}\), rotasyoneli \(2\hat{\mathbf k}\) ✓.

    5. (d) \(\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^{\pi/3}\!\!\int_0^a r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi=2\pi\cdot\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac{a^3}{3}=\boxed{\tfrac{\pi a^3}{3}}\).

    • (a) teğet vektörler ve diklik 4, \(h\) ve \(d\tau\) 2 · (b) 4 · (c) bileşenler 4, \(\hat{\mathbf k}\)'ye dönüşüm 2 · (d) 4
    B · Q3yüzey20 puan

    Surface integrals on a cone

    Q3

    \(S\) is the cone \(z=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\), \(0\le z\le2\).

    (a) Express \(d\sigma\) in terms of \(dx\,dy\) and find the area of \(S\). (b) Find the mass of \(S\) if its surface density is \(\sigma=x^2+y^2\). (c) Find the flux of \(\mathbf F=x\,\hat{\mathbf i}+y\,\hat{\mathbf j}+z\,\hat{\mathbf k}\) through \(S\). Explain the result geometrically.

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    \(g=z-\rho\), \(|\nabla g|=\sqrt2\)

    1. (a) \(\nabla g=(-x/\rho,\ -y/\rho,\ 1)\), \(|\nabla g|=\sqrt2\), \(|\partial_zg|=1\) ⇒ \(\boxed{d\sigma=\sqrt2\,dx\,dy}\). İzdüşüm \(\rho\le2\): alan \(\sqrt2\cdot4\pi=\boxed{4\sqrt2\,\pi}\).

    2. (b) \(M=\sqrt2\int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^2\rho^2\cdot\rho\,d\rho\,d\theta=\sqrt2\cdot2\pi\cdot4=\boxed{8\sqrt2\,\pi}\).

    3. (c) \(\mathbf F\cdot\nabla g=-x^2/\rho-y^2/\rho+z=-\rho+z=0\) koni üzerinde ⇒ akı \(\boxed0\) (hangi normal seçilirse seçilsin).

    4. Geometri: koni orijinden çıkan doğrulardan oluşur; \(\mathbf F=\mathbf r\) her noktada bu doğrular boyunca, yani yüzeye teğettir. Yüzeyden hiç alan çizgisi geçmez.

    • (a) \(d\sigma\) 4, alan 2 · (b) kurulum 4, sonuç 3 · (c) \(\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf n}=0\) 5, yorum 2
    B · Q4çizgi20 puan

    A field with a parameter

    Q4

    \(\mathbf G=(axy-z^3)\,\hat{\mathbf i}+(a-2)x^2\,\hat{\mathbf j}+(1-a)xz^2\,\hat{\mathbf k}\).

    (a) Find the constant \(a\) for which \(\mathbf G\) is conservative, and find the potential. (b) With that \(a\), evaluate \(\int_C\mathbf G\cdot d\mathbf r\) along \(C:\ \mathbf r(t)=(t,\ t^2,\ t^3)\), \(0\le t\le1\). (c) Evaluate the same integral directly for \(a=0\).

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    Üç rotasyonel bileşeni sıfıra eşitle

    1. (a) \(\partial_yG_x=\partial_xG_y\): \(ax=2(a-2)x\) ⇒ \(a=4\). \(\partial_zG_x=\partial_xG_z\): \(-3z^2=(1-a)z^2\) ⇒ \(a=4\) ✓. \(\partial_zG_y=0=\partial_yG_z\) ✓. Yani \(\boxed{a=4}\).

    Real integrals by residues

    Q5

    Use the residue theorem to find (a) \(J_1=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\dfrac{\cos x}{x^2+2x+2}dx\) and \(J_2=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\dfrac{\sin x}{x^2+2x+2}dx\); (b) \(J_3=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\dfrac{\sin x}{x(x^2+1)}dx\).

    Çözümü aç
    Çözüm

    Jordan; (b) için girinti

    1. (a) \(f=\frac{e^{iz}}{z^2+2z+2}\), kutuplar \(-1\pm i\); üstteki \(z_0=-1+i\): \(\mathrm{Res}=\frac{e^{iz_0}}{2i}=\frac{e^{-1}e^{-i}}{2i}\).

    2. \(J_1+iJ_2=2\pi i\,\mathrm{Res}=\frac\pi e(\cos1-i\sin1)\) ⇒ \(\boxed{J_1=\frac\pi e\cos1\approx0{,}624}\), \(\boxed{J_2=-\frac\pi e\sin1\approx-0{,}973}\).

    3. (b) \(g=\frac{e^{iz}}{z(z^2+1)}\): \(\mathrm{Res}_i=\frac{e^{-1}}{i\cdot2i}=-\frac{1}{2e}\), \(\mathrm{Res}_0=1\) (eksende). \(\mathrm{PV}\!\int g=2\pi i\big(-\frac{1}{2e}\big)+\pi i\cdot1=\pi i(1-e^{-1})\).

    4. \(J_3=\mathrm{Im}=\boxed{\pi(1-e^{-1})}\approx1{,}986\) (integrand \(x=0\)'da sonlu; PV gerçek integrale eşit).
