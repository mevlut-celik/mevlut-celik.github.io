The 35th Signal Processing and Communications Applications Conference (SIU 2027) will be held on July 4–7, 2027 at the Kavacık South Campus of Istanbul Medipol University. Held without interruption since 1993, SIU is the national conference bringing together researchers working in signal processing, communications, computer vision, machine learning and biomedical technologies.

Topics and Research Tracks

Track 1 — Communications and Networks: information theory and coding, communication theory and applications, wireless communications and networks, 5g/6g and beyond technologies and related topics.
Track 2 — Image Processing and Computer Vision: image and video processing, object detection and pattern recognition, multi-channel and multi-camera processing, image/video coding and compression and related topics.
Track 3 — Signal Processing and Applications: signal processing theory, statistical signal processing, nonlinear signal processing, audio/speech processing and related topics.
Track 4 — Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence: machine learning theory, deep learning, machine learning for healthcare, ml/dl for communication systems and related topics.
Track 5 — Biomedical Signal/Image Processing and Applications: biomedical signal analysis, medical image analysis and applications, bioinformatics and genomic signal processing, wearable sensors and e-health and related topics.
Track 6 — Natural Language Processing and Text Mining: large language models and training strategies, artificial intelligence and large language model agents, dialogue and interactive systems, information extraction and retrieval and related topics.
Track 7 — Robotics, Control and Automation: marine, air and land robots, industrial robots, humanoid and social robots, swarm robots and related topics.

Submission Rules

  • Language: papers must be written in Turkish. Papers written in English will be considered for review if one of the authors is not a native speaker of Turkish.
  • Length: at most 4 pages including references and figures, in the standard IEEE two-column conference format.
  • Originality: original work not previously published at another conference or in a journal.
  • Double-blind: author names, affiliations, e-mail addresses and acknowledgements must be removed from the initial submission, and PDF metadata cleared.

Where and How to Submit

Papers are accepted only through Microsoft CMT; submissions sent by e-mail will not be processed.

cmt3.research.microsoft.com/SIU2027

Publication and Copyright

Papers accepted and presented at the conference are submitted to the IEEE Xplore Digital Library. Publication requires an IEEE PDF eXpress check, the IEEE electronic copyright transfer (eCF) and registration of at least one author by May 24, 2027.

Special session and tutorial proposals: proposals must be sent to siu2027@medipol.edu.tr by November 16, 2026. Please follow the conference website for details and current announcements.
